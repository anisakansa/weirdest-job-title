## Entry

> **Professional Mourner for Strangers' Funerals** (44 characters)

Professional mourners are real — but mourning people you never met is the
gloriously odd twist. The title sounds like a real job, is specific enough
to picture the person doing it (black suit, front row, crying on cue at a
stranger's funeral), and the joke lands on the first read.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only, no network, no clock, no randomness)
writes the title to `/output/entry.txt` and verifies the round-trip
byte-for-byte. It enforces the gate itself: 1–60 UTF-8 characters, not
only whitespace.

Run: `node main.ts`
